Expected are the cars on their way there, entered and left count since the start, junctions also cars that arrive and start there. Speed, throughput and waiting are over the last minute: waiting is how long cars stood on a road before they left it, the queue the cars standing in a row at the end of the roads.

    Towns, roads and maps © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.

    • Open Shapes for crossings, traffic lights, roundabouts and roads with 1 to 3 lanes.
    • Drop the ends of a road on junctions, select two junctions and click a road, or drag from the arrows of a junction.
    • Select a junction or a road to change its type, green time and ring lanes, or the direction and turns of each lane, under Selection.
    • A road with one arrow is a one-way road, one with no or two arrows a road in both directions. lanes=2 in its style sets the lanes per direction.
    • Traffic lights give each approach axis 10 seconds of green (trafficLight=10), then 3 seconds of yellow.
    • Click a vehicle to see its route from A to B and its data, and click elsewhere to stop.
    • 1 unit is 1 meter and 1 step is 1 second.
