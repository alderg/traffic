Drawing Roads

  • Shapes are crossings, connectors between them are roads.
  • A connector with one arrow is a one-way road, one with no or two arrows a road in both directions.
  • Add trafficLight=10 to the style of a shape for a traffic light that switches every 10 seconds.
  • 1 unit is 1 meter and 1 step is 1 second.
